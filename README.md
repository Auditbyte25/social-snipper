# 🤖 AI-Powered Crypto Trading Assistant

An intelligent trading assistant that leverages social signals, influencer activity, and global news trends to identify early crypto opportunities while enforcing strong AI safety and user-controlled risk management.

---

## 🚀 Overview

This system analyzes:

- KOL (Key Opinion Leader) activity on social media (Twitter/X)
- Trending keywords from global news
- Market signals and sentiment patterns

It helps traders make informed decisions by combining:

- AI-driven insights
- Risk-aware constraints
- Human-in-the-loop control

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📡 Real-time ingestion of high-volume social media data (Twitter/X)
- 🧠 NLP-based sentiment and trend scoring
- 📊 Influencer credibility and engagement analysis
- 🪙 Token discovery and ranking engine
- 🛡️ AI safety & risk control mechanisms
- 🔍 Rug pull detection & token holder validation
- 👤 Human-in-the-loop trading decisions
- 🔐 Wallet-based authentication and subscription system
- 💰 Tiered access with referral support

---

## 🏗️ Architecture

- **Backend:** Node.js, TypeScript
- **Database:** MongoDB
- **AI/ML Layer:** Python (NLP models for sentiment and ranking)
- **Data Sources:** Twitter/X APIs, news feeds
- **Authentication:** Wallet-based access control
- **System Design:** Modular microservices-style architecture

---

## ⚙️ AI & ML Components

- Sentiment analysis of social posts
- Influencer credibility scoring
- Trend detection from keyword frequency
- Signal aggregation and ranking
- Token risk validation (rug checks, holder distribution)

---

## 🛡️ AI Safety & Risk Controls

The system is designed with safety as a core principle:

- User-defined:

  - Risk tolerance levels
  - Profit targets
  - Stop-loss limits
  - Market cap filters

- Automated safeguards:

  - Rug pull detection
  - Token holder analysis
  - Signal validation from multiple sources

- Human oversight:
  - AI acts as a decision-support assistant
  - Users retain final control over trades

## 🧪 Example Workflow

1. System collects tweets and news in real time
2. NLP models analyze sentiment and trends
3. Influencer signals are weighted and ranked
4. Tokens are validated (rug check + holder analysis)
5. Recommendations are generated
6. User reviews and decides to act
7. Risk controls enforce constraints
